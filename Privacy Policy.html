School-Edge-AI Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 13th January, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to School-Edge-AI. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, protect, and share your personal information when you use our mobile application. By using School-Edge, you agree to the terms of this privacy policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 User Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Role (Admin, Teacher, Student)
  • Contact information
  • Profile picture (optional)

2.2 Educational Information

  • Class details
  • Subject information
  • Attendance records
  • Assessment and quiz results
  • Academic performance data

2.3 Communication Data

  • Chat messages between users (students, teachers, admin)
  • Announcements
  • Notifications preferences

2.4 AI Assistant Interactions

  • Locally saved chat histories
  • AI-generated quiz questions and explanations
  • User interactions with AI features

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Purposes

  • Facilitate educational interactions
  • Manage class schedules and academic progress
  • Enable communication between users
  • Provide personalized learning experiences
  • Generate and manage academic content

3.2 Specific Feature Purposes

  • Schedule management
  • Attendance tracking
  • Assessment creation and grading
  • AI-assisted learning
  • Notification delivery
  • User authentication

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Data Storage

  • User data is stored securely on encrypted servers
  • Local device storage for chat histories and certain app data
  • Regular backups to prevent data loss

4.2 Security Measures

  • Encryption of sensitive information
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls based on user roles

5. Data Sharing

5.1 Internal Sharing

  • Administrators can view teacher and student data
  • Teachers can access student information for their assigned classes
  • Students can communicate with teachers and administrators

5.2 Third-Party Sharing

  • We do not sell personal information to third parties
  • Aggregate, anonymized data may be used for improving app functionality
  • Potential sharing with educational institutions with explicit consent

6. User Rights

6.1 Data Access and Control

  • Users can view and ask school administrator of the app to edit their personal information
  • Ability to modify notification preferences

6.2 Consent and Withdrawal

  • Option to request account deletion and associated data by contacting the school administrator

7. AI Features and Data

7.1 AI Interaction

AI-generated content is for educational purposes only. Chat histories are saved locally with user consent, and no personal identifiable information is used to train AI models.

To deliver AI-generated responses, School-Edge-AI integrates with trusted third-party API providers, including:

  • Gemini Flash 3B
  • Claude-Haiku
  • Nvidia Nemotron
  • Mistral

8. Child Privacy and Safety

School-Edge AI is designed for users aged 13 and above. We are committed to providing a safe and age-appropriate experience for young users.

Age-Appropriate Content

AI-generated content is strictly related to educational subtopics and is carefully curated to avoid inappropriate or irrelevant responses.

Thorough Prompt Review

All prompts are thoroughly reviewed and rectified within our system before being sent to third-party API providers.

No Direct Data Collection from Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13.

Parental or Guardian Consent

Users between 13 and 18 years of age must obtain consent from a parent or guardian before using our application.

9. Compliance

  • Adheres to educational data protection regulations
  • GDPR and COPPA compliant
  • Regular updates to maintain compliance

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

  • We may update this policy periodically
  • Users will be notified of significant changes
  • Continued use implies acceptance of updated terms
